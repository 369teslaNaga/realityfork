02 / Rewrite the rules

GRAVITY

Planet values are approximate gravity ratios. Jupiter represents a reference acceleration, not a solid landing surface.

TimeFork changes playback rate; it doesn't change gravity, velocity, or physical laws.

Restitution represents one ball–surface collision pair. Material names are illustrative presets, not universal measurements.

Wind is represented as horizontal acceleration, not a computational fluid simulation.

pixels / metre

Enter measured pixels per metre from a known reference in the camera plane. Without calibration, observed coordinates use fractions of the frame; physical units are not claimed.

THE IDEA / OPEN SCIENCE

Reality is the starting point.
Curiosity is the engine.

Most tools help you watch what happened. RealityFork lets you ask “what if?” — and inspect the assumptions behind the answer.

Open-source, reproducible and privacy-first. Synthetic worlds are labeled. User videos stay local. Every “fork” should make science easier to question, not harder to trust.
