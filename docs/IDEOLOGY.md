# The RealityFork Ideology

## The question

A camera records **what happened**. A simulation asks **what if the rules had been different**. RealityFork bridges those experiences, without pretending that model predictions are experimental observations.

We are not creating another generic AI chatbot, a fake 3D digital twin, or a website that transforms video using a paid generative AI API. We want a small, understandable scientific tool that produces a visually astonishing outcome *and* lets a user inspect the mathematics.

## One sentence

> RealityFork is an open-source laboratory where reality can be observed, questioned, forked, compared and shared.

## Product philosophy

1. **No gatekeeping:** a curious school student and a senior research engineer should be able to open the same free page.
2. **Not a black box:** the model equations and source code must be inspectable, with fixed-step deterministic tests.
3. **Evidence before confidence:** show measured pixels, calibration assumptions and tracking weaknesses plainly.
4. **The wow moment matters:** synthetic ball + Moon gravity should make the difference obvious in ten seconds.
5. **Share discoveries, not secrets:** public links serialize settings, not personal video or private tracks.
6. **Build with the world:** contributors can extend physical scenarios, support more measurement methods and design transparent experiments.

## Distinctive positioning

Existing motion trackers help measure motion. General physics sandboxes let you invent motion. **Our positioning** combines a local observed-source workflow with instantly editable, reproducible counterfactual worlds and built-in scientific candor. Other software may share these capabilities: RealityFork does not claim to be the first-ever implementation.

## Why the six modules belong together

GravityFork changes acceleration. TimeFork lets a user inspect the sequence. MaterialFork examines impacts. WindFork examines horizontal forcing and drag. MotionDNA exposes the underlying measurements and model outputs. RealityShare allows the experiment to travel as a reproducible artifact.

## First public release / brand

- **Name:** RealityFork
- **Voice:** precise, curious, daring, humble about uncertainty
- **Tagline:** Change the laws. Fork reality.
- **Short hook:** I filmed a ball. Then I gave it Moon gravity.
- **GitHub About text:** Film motion. Change gravity, time, bounce and wind. Compare alternate 2D physics in your browser. 100% local-first and open source.
- **Repo Topics:** `physics`, `simulation`, `video-analysis`, `open-science`, `javascript`, `browser`, `motion-tracking`, `educational`, `local-first`, `counterfactual`
- **Audience:** developers, educators, students, physics enthusiasts, designers of reproducible experiments

## Non-negotiables

Never imply real-time arbitrary-video understanding, 3D reconstruction, fully physical video editing, a calibrated result without suitable reference measurements, or online hosting when it hasn't happened. Viral attention should follow from an authentic and reproducible surprise, not fabricated metrics.

## Long-term ambition

RealityFork could evolve into a platform where others contribute benchmark videos, scene models, new laws, experiments and comparable physics engines. Achieving that requires open benchmarks, reproducible science, more robust visual tracking and community trust; this beta is only the beginning.
